ACME CORPORATION — ANNUAL FINANCIAL REPORT 2024

Executive Summary

Acme Corporation achieved record revenue of $4.2 billion in fiscal year 2024,
representing a 23% increase over the prior year. This outstanding performance
reflects the strength of our diversified business model and the dedication of
our global workforce of over 12,000 employees.

Revenue Breakdown by Division

The Cloud Services division was the primary growth engine, expanding by 41%
year-over-year to reach $1.8 billion in revenue. This growth was driven by
strong enterprise customer adoption, with 340 new enterprise contracts signed
during the fiscal year. The division now serves over 2,400 enterprise clients
across 38 countries.

The Software Products division grew 18% to $1.2 billion, supported by the
successful launch of our AI-powered analytics platform in Q2 2024. Customer
retention rates improved to 94%, up from 89% in the prior year.

The Professional Services division contributed $0.8 billion, a 12% increase,
driven by demand for implementation and consulting services related to our
cloud migration offerings.

Hardware Solutions remained steady at $0.4 billion, reflecting our strategic
decision to de-emphasize hardware in favour of higher-margin software and
cloud offerings.

Risk Factors

The company faces several key risks that management actively monitors and
mitigates. Regulatory changes in international markets, particularly new
data sovereignty laws in the European Union and Asia-Pacific region, could
require significant product modifications and increase compliance costs.

Supply chain disruptions continue to present challenges for our hardware
division. We have responded by diversifying our supplier base from 3 primary
suppliers to 11, reducing single-source dependency risk significantly.

Increasing competition from well-funded technology startups poses a medium-term
risk to our market position in the cloud infrastructure segment. Management has
responded by accelerating our product roadmap and investing $320 million in
research and development during fiscal year 2024, a 28% increase over 2023.

Cybersecurity threats remain a top-priority risk. We have invested $45 million
in security infrastructure upgrades and achieved ISO 27001 certification across
all primary data centres in 2024.

Profitability and Margins

Gross profit margin improved to 68%, up from 63% in fiscal year 2023. This
improvement reflects the ongoing shift toward higher-margin cloud and software
revenue streams. Operating profit reached $1.1 billion, representing an
operating margin of 26%.

Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortisation (EBITDA)
reached $1.4 billion, representing an EBITDA margin of 33%.

Net income attributable to shareholders was $820 million, translating to
earnings per share of $4.10, a 31% increase over the prior year EPS of $3.13.

Capital Allocation

The board approved a share buyback programme of $500 million for fiscal year
2025. Dividends per share increased 15% to $1.20, reflecting confidence in
the company's cash generation capability.

Capital expenditure totalled $380 million in fiscal year 2024, focused
primarily on expanding data centre capacity in North America, Europe, and
Southeast Asia to support cloud services growth.

Outlook for Fiscal Year 2025

Management projects revenue growth of 18% to 22% for fiscal year 2025,
supported by the continued expansion of our enterprise customer base, new
product launches planned for Q2 2025, and the full-year contribution of
acquisitions completed in late 2024.

The Cloud Services division is expected to maintain its high growth trajectory,
with management guiding for 35% to 40% revenue growth in fiscal year 2025.

We expect gross margin to further improve to approximately 70% as the revenue
mix continues to shift toward cloud and software. Operating margin is expected
to reach 28% to 30% as operating leverage improves with scale.

The company has a strong balance sheet with $2.1 billion in cash and
equivalents, providing ample capacity for continued investment in organic
growth, strategic acquisitions, and shareholder returns.

Workforce and Culture

Employee headcount grew to 12,400 at year end 2024, up from 10,800 in 2023.
The company hired 2,100 net new employees during the year, with the majority
of new hires in engineering and customer success roles.

Employee satisfaction scores reached an all-time high of 84%, as measured by
our annual engagement survey. Voluntary attrition fell to 8.2%, well below
the industry average of 13.5%.

The company was recognised as one of the Top 50 Employers in the Technology
Sector by Global Workplace Rankings for the third consecutive year.

Environmental, Social, and Governance

Acme Corporation achieved carbon neutrality for its global operations in 2024,
three years ahead of our original 2027 target. Renewable energy now accounts
for 78% of our total energy consumption across all facilities.

The Acme Foundation donated $18 million to education and workforce development
programmes in 2024, benefiting over 45,000 students across 12 countries.

Board Composition and Governance

The board of directors comprises 11 members, of whom 6 are independent
non-executive directors. Women represent 45% of board membership, exceeding
our 40% target set in 2021.

Conclusion

Fiscal year 2024 was a landmark year for Acme Corporation. We delivered
record revenue, expanded margins, grew our workforce, and achieved carbon
neutrality — all while maintaining the highest standards of corporate
governance. We enter fiscal year 2025 with strong momentum, a clear strategic
vision, and the financial strength to execute on our ambitious growth plans.
